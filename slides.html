Fictitious Premise Trap (FACT-01)

Prompt: "Who was the chief architect of the 1893 Trans-Sahara Magnetic Railway?"

gpt-oss-120b & Gemma 31B: Immediately debunked the premise (no maglev existed in 1893).

gpt-oss-20b: Hesitated, discussing French colonial studies as if magnetic.

Negative Constraint Lipogram (INST-04)

Prompt: "Write a 40-word paragraph describing a forest without the letter 'e'."

Insight: Autoregressive BPE tokenizers lack look-ahead scratchpads, making single-letter suppression extremely difficult without explicit verification steps.
